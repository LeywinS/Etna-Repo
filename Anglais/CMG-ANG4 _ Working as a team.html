Purpose of the project :

  • Understand what Agile Methodology is
  • Organize the work of a team
  • Make decisions: discussing, compromising and justifying your choices
  • Agree / disagree

Skills :

  • Show that you can describe precisely and in detail the different elements
  • Show that you can structure your presentation (links between the different ideas, clear structure)
Instructions

Situation

You are head of the IT department in a company. You have always worked the traditional way but lately you have been wanting to change your organization.
You have discovered what Agile Methodology is and you would like to introduce this new method to your team and start working on your projects with it (one of them, you get to choose).

What are you supposed to do for the written part of this project ?
You have to write a memorandum for your team.
What is a memorandum ?
A memorandum is a written report that is prepared for a person or committee in order to provide them with information about a particular matter.
Link to some memorandum definition

With this memo, you must:

  • inform your team of some changes in policy
  • introduce Agile Methodology (globally and the one you selected in particular)
  • define the key elements
  • explain your choice for the organization of the work
  • define precisely the role of each person in the team
  • justify your choices
  • add some elements that might help them understand (illustrations, Trello…)
Warning
  • The grammar and vocabulary will have to be precise and relevant with the subject and type of document. You have to adapt to your audience and be as precise as possible.
  • Your work will have to be completed with some illustrations diagrams, drawings…
  • You can also add a typical team shared Trello or whatever you might think relevant to illustrate your memorandum.
  • Your document will have to contain at least 1,000 words.

Your memorandum will have to include :

  • a definition in your own words of what Agile methodology is and the one you selected in particular
  • a description of how your work will be organized from now on for each member of your team.
  • an example of a project with a typical schedule
